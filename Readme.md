## 🍃 Overview

**Samanvay (समनवय)** is a digital mindful sanctuary crafted to help you slow down, ground your awareness, and release accumulated emotional weight. Unlike traditional games that rely on timers, scores, competition, or addictive loops, *Samanvay* has no losing conditions, no rush, and zero anxiety.

You gently roll a peaceful dice and stroll through 24 serene tiles—each offering a breath, a moment of reflection, heart cleansing, or gratitude.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **🌿 Soft Watercolor & Glassmorphism Aesthetic:** A calming visual palette of sage greens, pale teal, soft lotus rose, and natural paper textures with gently floating leaves.
- **🔔 Procedural Web Audio Soundscape:** Real-time synthesis of authentic Tibetan singing bowl frequencies (432 Hz), healing chimes (528 Hz), and optional continuous ambient drone synthesizer—**100% offline with zero external audio assets**.
- **🧘 24 Mindful Sanctuaries:**
  - **Thodi Saans (Deep Breath):** An interactive 4-2-4 circular breathing orb visualizer synchronized with gentle sound chimes.
  - **Apne Saath (Self-Reflection):** Heartfulness-aligned contemplations on letting go, forgiving oneself, building resilience, and transcending betrayal.
  - **Nirmalta (Heart Cleaning):** A 15-second guided visualization to consciously discharge emotional smoke and heavy burdens from the back.
  - **Shukriya (Gratitude Notepad):** A private reflection space where your written thoughts gently dissolve into glowing lotus petals floating into the sky.
  - **Prakriti (Nature Contemplation):** Gentle grounding reminders inspired by trees, flowing rivers, and natural renewal.
  - **Shanti (Goal):** A loving closure sanctuary with a lotus blooming animation and peaceful blessings.
- **🔒 100% Private & Offline:** Everything runs entirely inside your browser. No data leaves your device, no cookies, no tracking.

## 🎯 How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser on your phone, tablet, or desktop.
2. Click **"Roll Peaceful Dice"** to move your token 1 to 4 spaces forward.
3. Arrive at a sanctuary and take your time to read, breathe, or write.
4. Toggle ambient drone sounds or sound effects anytime from the top controls.
5. Reach the **Shanti Sanctuary** to complete your inner circuit.
